Optional hint: what makes an issue entry useful?

A useful entry identifies the specific gap, explains why it matters to the electrical design, names the discipline or person best placed to resolve it, and gives a concrete follow-up action. It is okay to mark an item as unresolved when the information is not available.

Optional hint: preliminary calculations

Begin with an input list and identify which values are confirmed versus pending. Document the calculation basis and units. Do not use incomplete equipment data as if it were final manufacturer data, and don't confuse a preliminary estimate with a verified load calculation.
